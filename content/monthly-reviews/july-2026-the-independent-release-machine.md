July was a month of movement. Rolling Stone India’s release roundups tracked artists moving between rap, electronic music, indie rock and regional-language pop with unusually little concern for genre boundaries.

Ritviz continued the rollout around his upcoming *Kurta Saree* project, Haniya Nafisa arrived with her debut album *Vaakkath*, Paal Dabba kept his rapid-release rhythm going, and the Malayalam underground gained another visible bridge through the *Kerala Underground* anthem featuring Dabzee, The Imbachi, Parimal Shais and MC Couper.

The month also brought a high-profile contradiction: Arijit Singh, who had said earlier in 2026 that he was stepping away from playback projects, appeared on Amaal Mallik’s “Yeh Awarapan” for *Awarapan 2*.

### The bigger picture

The release cycle is becoming more flexible. Artists can build an audience through singles, collaborations, regional scenes and live communities, then let the larger project arrive later.

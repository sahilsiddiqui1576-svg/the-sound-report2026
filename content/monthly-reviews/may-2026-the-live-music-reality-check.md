The live-music story in May was less about another record-breaking concert and more about resilience. Reporting in Business Standard documented how the West Asia conflict disrupted several big-ticket shows and travel plans, even as India’s music-tourism market continued to expand.

That tension matters. Fans are increasingly willing to travel for artists, festivals and premium experiences, but the ecosystem around those events remains vulnerable to flight disruption, venue capacity, insurance, clearances and international touring schedules.

At the same time, independent music kept proving that live discovery does not need a stadium. Smaller rooms, city circuits and artist-led communities remain critical to how audiences find new acts.

### The May read

The Indian live market is maturing, but it is not frictionless. The opportunity is enormous; the infrastructure still has to catch up.

The April conversation was shaped by a new set of numbers from the FICCI-EY 2026 media and entertainment report. Music-label revenue from licensing and other income reached **₹59 billion in 2025**, with **58% coming from digital licensing**. Streaming reached roughly **178 million listeners and 5.8 trillion streams**, while paid subscribers reached 14 million.

The more interesting story is what those numbers say about the shape of listening. **57% of music consumption was film-based**, but Hindi’s share slipped to 59%, with Punjabi at 9%, Tamil at 7% and Telugu at 6%. More than 40% of the top 50 songs were older Hindi-film releases, suggesting that catalogue is not a side business in India. It is the backbone.

### What we’re watching

- Paid streaming is becoming more important to the economics of music.
- Catalogue continues to compete with new releases for attention.
- Regional-language listening is gaining share without needing to displace Hindi overnight.
- The next phase of growth will be about who captures value, not simply who gets streams.

**Reporting note:** Data points in this review are drawn from the FICCI-EY 2026 report as reported in April 2026.

Two launches stood out in August. Yash Raj Films announced **Raah Records**, positioning it as a music company and incubator for artists outside the film space. Its first release was Aman’s “Jaadugari.”

Believe India also launched **BL/UR**, a pop imprint that followed its regional-focused Neue, Mahra Tora and Think Indie initiatives. The first BL/UR release, Anurag Saikia’s “Bandeela,” drew on Rajasthani folk colour while sitting inside a contemporary pop framework.

The message is difficult to miss: labels are no longer treating non-film music as one undifferentiated bucket. They are building specialised pipelines around language, genre and artist identity.

### August takeaway

The new gatekeepers are not necessarily replacing the old ones. They are multiplying. For artists, that can mean more routes in, but also more competition for attention.

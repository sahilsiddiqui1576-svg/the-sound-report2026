September opened with one of the clearest data points of the year. Spotify’s India edition of **Loud & Clear**, released on September 2, reported that royalties generated by Indian artists grew **29% year over year in 2025**, with nearly **335 billion streams** on the platform.

More strikingly, **more than 40% of royalties earned by Indian artists came from listeners outside India**. Regional growth was even sharper: Telugu royalties rose more than 120%, Marathi nearly 50%, Bengali nearly 40%, Malayalam more than 30%, Hindi nearly 30% and Tamil 20%. Haryanvi streams were nearly seven times higher in February 2026 than in February 2023.

Independent music is central to the shift. Eleven of Spotify’s 15 most-exported Indian songs in 2025 came directly from artists rather than film soundtracks.

### September’s thesis

India is no longer simply becoming a bigger music market. It is becoming a larger **source market** for global listening.

That changes what success looks like: a song can be culturally local, digitally native and commercially global at the same time.

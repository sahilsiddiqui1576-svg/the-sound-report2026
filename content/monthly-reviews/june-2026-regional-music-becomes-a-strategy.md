June brought a significant signal from the label side: **Warner Music India announced a strategic partnership with SVF Entertainment to expand its Bengali music business**, building on its presence across South Indian, Punjabi, Haryanvi and Bhojpuri markets.

This is more than a regional-content story. Labels are increasingly treating language markets as growth engines with their own audiences, creators, catalogues and export potential.

The independent ecosystem offered another clue. Misfits Inc.’s *Not Dead Yet – it’s aLive! The Compilation, Vol. 1* collected 22 live recordings from Mumbai’s antiSOCIAL, bringing together artists across genres, regions and languages.

### Why it matters

India’s next music map will not be one national chart with regional sidebars. It will be a network of scenes that can break locally, travel digitally and attract investment from outside their home markets.

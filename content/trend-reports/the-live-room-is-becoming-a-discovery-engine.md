The 22-track *Not Dead Yet – it’s aLive!* compilation, recorded across two years at Mumbai’s antiSOCIAL, is a useful snapshot of the role live venues now play in discovery.

The project brings together artists from different genres, regions and languages, preserving the atmosphere of a room rather than reducing every performance to a single polished studio track.

As streaming becomes more crowded, offline discovery becomes more valuable. The live room is not just where an artist performs. It is where a community forms, a promoter discovers talent and a fan learns what to listen to next.

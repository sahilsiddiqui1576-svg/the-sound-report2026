For years, “global Indian music” was treated as a handful of crossover exceptions. The latest Spotify data suggests something broader. More than 40% of royalties generated by Indian artists on Spotify in 2025 came from listeners outside India.

Eleven of the 15 most-exported Indian songs came directly from artists rather than film soundtracks. That is a meaningful change in the export equation: artists do not need to wait for a movie to give a song an international distribution moment.

The new challenge is conversion. Discovery abroad is growing, but touring, publishing, merchandise and fan communities have to follow if streaming reach is going to become durable business.

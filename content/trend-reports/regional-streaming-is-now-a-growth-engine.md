Spotify’s latest India Loud & Clear report is the clearest data point yet for a trend the industry has been watching: regional-language music is becoming one of the strongest growth layers in India’s streaming economy.

Royalties generated by Telugu music grew more than 120% year over year in 2025, Marathi nearly 50%, Bengali nearly 40%, Malayalam more than 30% and Tamil 20%. Haryanvi streams were nearly seven times higher in February 2026 than in February 2023.

The key change is infrastructure. Discovery tools, editorial playlists and local-language programming are making it easier for listeners to find music outside the biggest Hindi and English ecosystems.

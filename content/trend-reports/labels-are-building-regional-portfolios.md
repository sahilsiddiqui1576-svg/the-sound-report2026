Warner Music India’s June partnership with SVF Entertainment to expand in Bengali music fits into a larger pattern. Major companies are building dedicated regional strategies instead of treating every non-Hindi market as a single bucket.

Believe India’s portfolio makes the same point from another angle, with separate imprints around Punjabi, Haryanvi, Tamil and now broader pop.

For artists, this can mean better local marketing and stronger language expertise. For labels, it creates a portfolio of scenes with different audience behaviours and export potential.

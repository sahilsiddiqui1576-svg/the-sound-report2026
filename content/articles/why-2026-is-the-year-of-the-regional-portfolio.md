Warner Music India’s Bengali partnership with SVF Entertainment and Believe India’s growing family of regional imprints point to the same business idea: localisation is becoming a competitive advantage.

The opportunity is not simply more releases. It is better A&R, marketing, partnerships and catalogue strategy for individual scenes.

For artists, the best version of this model could mean that a regional identity is an asset rather than a barrier. The challenge will be keeping local creative control while scaling beyond the home market.

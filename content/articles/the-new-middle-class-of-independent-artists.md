The FICCI-EY 2026 report showed a music market becoming more digital and more valuable, while Spotify’s latest India report shows the number of artists crossing meaningful annual royalty thresholds is also growing.

That suggests the emergence of a new middle class of independent artists: not global superstars, but creators with enough repeat listeners to build businesses around touring, publishing, sync, merchandise and community.

The next five years may be less about finding the next overnight sensation and more about making these mid-sized careers sustainable.

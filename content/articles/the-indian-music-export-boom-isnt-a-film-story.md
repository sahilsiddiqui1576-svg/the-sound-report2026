The most important number in Spotify’s September 2026 India Loud & Clear report may be the one that sits outside the headline: **more than 40% of royalties generated by Indian artists came from listeners outside India** in 2025.

That changes the old definition of crossover. The export story is no longer limited to a handful of film songs, diaspora audiences or a major-label push. Independent artists are increasingly able to travel through playlists, social discovery and algorithmic recommendation.

Eleven of the 15 most-exported Indian songs on Spotify’s 2025 Global Impact List came directly from artists rather than film soundtracks. That is the clearest signal yet that India’s independent music ecosystem is becoming export infrastructure in its own right.

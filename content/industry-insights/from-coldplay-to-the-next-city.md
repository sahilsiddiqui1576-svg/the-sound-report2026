India’s live entertainment market was valued at **₹20,861 crore in 2024** and has been growing at roughly 15% annually, according to the Ministry of Information & Broadcasting’s Joint Working Group materials. The government wants India to become one of the world’s top five live-entertainment destinations by 2030.

The next growth phase will depend on more than headline concerts. Tier-2 cities, venue development, licensing, transport and skilled event crews will determine whether the market can scale without making every tour dependent on the same few metros.

*Mirzapur: The Movie* became the first Bollywood movie soundtrack to be released behind a paywall, according to Billboard India’s September 4 industry roundup. The experiment gives paying subscribers a 72-hour first window under Universal Music Group’s Hear It First initiative.

The model is interesting because film music has historically depended on scale and free discovery. Early access reverses that logic: scarcity becomes the product.

The experiment will be worth watching because the success metric is not simply first-day streams. It is whether a premium window can create enough urgency to change listener behaviour.

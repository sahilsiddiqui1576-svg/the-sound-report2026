Spotify says Indian artists generated nearly **335 billion streams in 2025**, while the number of artists generating more than ₹10 million annually grew 21% year over year. The number generating more than ₹50 million annually has doubled since 2023.

Those numbers do not mean streaming has solved artist economics. They do show that the pool of commercially successful artists is broadening.

The business question for 2026 is what happens after the stream: can artists convert listening into touring, publishing, fan memberships, sync, merchandise and long-term catalogue value?
